Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

Information we collect

We collect the account, profile, contact, and appointment information you provide when using Clinica Legarda. This may include your name, email address, phone number, appointment details, and notes needed to provide clinic services.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, send one-time passwords and account messages, schedule and manage appointments, and support clinic operations.

Google and Gmail data

Clinica Legarda uses the Gmail API only to send one-time passwords and account-related email from the clinic account. The application does not read, download, or manage a user's Gmail inbox.

Storage and sharing

Application data is stored in the clinic application's database and is available only to the roles that need it to operate the service. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your application information by contacting us. Some information may need to be retained where it is required to provide or document clinic services.
